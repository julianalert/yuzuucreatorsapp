## comments
Paste 10-20 audience comments. Open their 3-4 most-commented posts and take the
ones where people describe their own problem.

This is the highest-value input in the whole file. The audience's words for their
own problem are what make stage 1 good, and nothing else in the pipeline can
recover them if they're missing.

Take comments verbatim, typos and all. Don't clean them up — the messiness is
signal. Skip pure praise ("love this!") and anything with a name in it.

## expectation
Write your prediction BEFORE you run it. One line.

Examples: "should pass, very diagnostic niche" / "low segmentability, expect the
pipeline to reject every topic" / "health adjacent, want to see disclaimers".

Writing this down first is what turns the run into an experiment instead of a
vibe check. When the result surprises you, that's the finding.
